## How to use this list

- `[x]` means completed and accepted with evidence. `[ ]` means pending acceptance; an unreviewed partial draft may exist.
- Mark at most one task `IN PROGRESS`. If work cannot continue, add `BLOCKED — reason` to that task; do not check it off.
- Keep task numbers stable. At completion, update the checkbox and link relevant tests or [validation evidence](docs/validation.md). Update **Current focus** and **Next task** in the same change.
- Add newly discovered work under the relevant milestone. Update specifications when scope changes; do not duplicate progress lists in other documents.
- Milestones are completion gates, not deadlines. Prototype behavior never counts as native integration evidence.
